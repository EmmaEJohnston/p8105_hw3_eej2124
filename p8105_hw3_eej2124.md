## Import, clean, and tidy the data

``` r
demographic_df =
  read_csv("data/nhanes_demographics.csv",
           skip = 4,
           na = c("", "NA", ".")) |> 
  janitor::clean_names() |> 
  drop_na() |> 
  filter(age >= 21) |> 
  mutate(
    sex = case_match(
      sex, 
     1 ~ c("male"), 
     2 ~ c("female")), 
    education = case_match(
      education, 
      1 ~ c("less than high school"), 
      2 ~ c("high school equivalent"), 
      3 ~ c("more than high school")
    ))
```
